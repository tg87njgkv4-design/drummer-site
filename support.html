What gear works?

A full kit, an electronic kit, a snare and hi-hat, or just a pair of sticks, with or without a practice pad. Every exercise uses only the pieces you have. You need an iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and no account.

How do the tempos change?

After each set, rate it Clean, Almost or Not yet. Two Clean in a row, at the exercise’s tempo or faster, move it up one step next time; two Not yet in a row, at its tempo or slower, move it down one step; Almost holds it. An exercise moves at most one step per workout, and your best is the fastest tempo you’ve rated Clean.

What’s the card on my Lock Screen?

While a workout or the Metronome is running, a Live Activity shows the exercise, the set, the tempo and the time played on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. You can pause a workout or stop the Metronome without unlocking your phone; resuming may open the app. If it doesn’t show, turn on Live Activities for Drummer in the iPhone’s Settings, under Drummer.

Can I play along with music?

Yes. During a workout, music from another app keeps playing with the click. On the Metronome tab, turn on Play along to do the same.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app: touch and hold its icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Everything Drummer saved goes with it. To delete one finished workout, open it from your practice history and tap Delete workout.

More questions

How is my program built?

In setup you choose your gear, your level (Beginner, Intermediate, Developed or Professional), your style (Rock, Pop, Jazz, R&B, or Not sure yet), how many days a week you practice and how long each workout is. Drummer builds a week of named workouts, each exercise set out as sets and bars at a tempo. You can change your setup any time in Settings.

Does Drummer listen to me play?

No. Drummer doesn’t use the microphone. You rate each set yourself.

Is my data backed up or synced?

Your program and history are saved on your iPhone only, with no account and no syncing between devices. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Drummer’s data is part of that backup, and restoring it brings it back.

Where can I read how it all works?

In the app: tap the profile button at the top right of any tab, then How it works.
