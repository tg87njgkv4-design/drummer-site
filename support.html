Drummer

Support

Drummer is a drum practice app for iPhone: a weekly program built around your gear and level, a metronome, and your progress, measured by the tempos you play clean.

Have a question, found a problem, or have an idea? Write to us, and tell us your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn’t working.

Email getdrummerapp@gmail.com

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and one of: a full drum kit, an electronic kit, a snare and hi-hat, or just a pair of sticks (with or without a practice pad). Exercises only use the pieces you have. No account is needed.

How is my program built?

When you set up the app, you choose your gear, your level (Beginner, Intermediate, Developed or Professional), the style you play (Rock, Pop, Jazz, R&B, or Not sure yet), how many days a week you practice and how long each workout is. Drummer builds a weekly program from that, with each day named and each exercise set out as sets and bars at a tempo. You can change your setup any time in Settings.

How do the tempos change?

After each set, you rate it Clean, Almost or Not yet. Two Clean sets in a row, at the exercise’s tempo or faster, move it up one step next time; two Not yet in a row, at its tempo or slower, move it down one step; Almost holds it where it is. An exercise moves at most one step per workout, and always stays within its own range. Your best is the fastest tempo you’ve rated Clean. How it works, on the profile screen (the button at the top right of each tab), explains this in full.

The Live Activity doesn’t show

Check that Live Activities are on for Drummer in the iPhone’s Settings, under Drummer. They show while a workout or the Metronome is running, on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island on iPhones that have one.

Can I play along with music?

Yes. In the Metronome, turn on Play along, and the click plays alongside music from another app instead of stopping it.

Does Drummer listen to me play?

No. Drummer doesn’t use the microphone. You rate each set yourself.

Is my data backed up or synced?

Your program and history are saved on your iPhone only. There’s no account and no syncing between devices. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Drummer’s data is part of that backup, and restoring the backup brings it back.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app: touch and hold its icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Everything Drummer saved goes with it. To delete a single finished workout, open it from your practice history and tap Delete workout.

Privacy

Drummer collects no data: no accounts, no tracking, no analytics, and no internet connection. Read the privacy policy.
