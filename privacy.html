Drummer

Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Drummer doesn’t collect any data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone, and the app doesn’t connect to the internet.

What the app keeps, and where

To build your program and follow your progress, Drummer saves these on your iPhone only:

  • Your setup answers: your gear, level, style, days per week and workout length.
  • Your program and where you are in it, including a paused workout.
  • Your finished workouts, the rating you gave each set (Clean, Almost or Not yet), your tempos and your bests.
  • Your Metronome settings.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We can’t see it, and we have no server to send it to.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, iOS includes Drummer’s saved data in that backup, as it does for most apps. That backup is handled by Apple and your own devices, not by Drummer.

No internet connection

Drummer has no networking code. It doesn’t download or upload anything, and it uses no third-party services, frameworks for analytics or advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Microphone, contacts, location and photos

Drummer doesn’t ask for access to your microphone, camera, contacts, location, photos or health data, and doesn’t use them. It only plays sound.

Phone calls

So a workout or the Metronome can pause when a call comes in, Drummer checks whether a call is ringing or connected, using iOS’s standard call status. It doesn’t see who is calling, any phone numbers or your call history, and it keeps nothing about the call.

Live Activities

While a workout or the Metronome is running, Drummer can show a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island: the exercise, the set, the tempo and the time played, or the Metronome’s tempo, with buttons to pause, resume, stop or start. It’s drawn by your iPhone from information inside the app; nothing is sent over the internet. Anyone who can see your Lock Screen can see it. You can turn Live Activities off for Drummer in the iPhone’s Settings, under Drummer.

Sharing a workout

After a workout you can tap Share. Drummer hands a picture of the workout summary and one line of text (for example “Drummer: Day 3 · Hands & Coordination, Thursday, 12 November. 24 min, 11 sets, 7 Clean, 1 new best.”) to your iPhone’s own share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where it goes, and what happens next is up to the app or person you send it to. Drummer itself sends nothing.

Deleting your data

To delete everything Drummer has saved, delete the app: touch and hold its icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Its data is removed from your iPhone with it. Copies in earlier iPhone backups stay in those backups until they’re replaced or deleted.

Inside the app you can also delete a single finished workout: open it from your practice history and tap Delete workout.

Children

Drummer collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Crash reports from Apple

If you’ve chosen in your iPhone’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics about Drummer. This is Apple’s choice to offer and yours to turn on or off, under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements. Drummer itself sends nothing.

If you email us

If you write to support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to answer you, and don’t add it to any list.
