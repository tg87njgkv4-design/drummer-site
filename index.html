Drum workouts built around the gear you have.

A weekly practice plan for your level and style, a metronome, and tempos that move with your own ratings.

What you get

  • Built around your gear

    A full kit, an electronic kit, a snare and hi-hat, or just sticks. Every exercise uses only the pieces you have.

  • Your level, your style

    Beginner to Professional, in Rock, Pop, Jazz or R&B, on the days and for the time you choose.

  • Tempos that follow your ratings

    Rate each set Clean, Almost or Not yet. Two clean sets in a row move that exercise up a step next time.

  • A metronome

    Time signatures, subdivisions, count-in and tap tempo, with Play along to keep the click over your music.

  • On your Lock Screen

    The set, the tempo and the time played. Pause a workout or stop the Metronome without unlocking your iPhone.

Who is it for?

Drummers at every level, from beginners to professionals. Practice on a full kit, an electronic kit, a snare and hi-hat, or just a pair of sticks, with or without a practice pad. No account needed.
